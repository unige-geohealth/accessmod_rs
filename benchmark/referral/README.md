# Referral benchmark

AccessMod 5 against accessmod-rs, all facilities to all facilities
(pairs = N × (N − 1)). Results: [`report.md`](report.md), generated by:

```sh
sh benchmark/referral/run.sh [side facilities ...]   # default: 1000 20 2000 50 4000 100
```

Needs Docker, the AccessMod 5 image `fredmoser/accessmod:5.9.2-beta.6` and,
for the demo part, the oracle exports (`sh scripts/oracle/export.sh`).
`REPEAT` (default 3) sets the runs per measurement; the report gives the
median. Scratch files go to `_shared/benchmark/referral/`.

## What is measured

**Demo project, full analysis.** The AccessMod 5 demo (227 × 196 cells,
10 facilities, 90 pairs, anisotropic, 8 neighbours):

- AccessMod 5: the replay `scripts/oracle/referral-demo.json` (from
  `replay/examples/demo_referral`, seconds and metres) run by
  `amAnalysisReplayExec`: one travel time per origin, `r.path` from every
  destination, vector network and distances, tables. Loading `global.R` is
  reported apart.
- accessmod-rs: `referral run` on the same data (`demo-job.json`): times
  and distances of all pairs, tables and reports.

**Synthetic landscapes, engine.** `side × side` cells of 100 m from
`synthetic_inputs`, `facilities` facilities on the main road, the scenario
of [`../scenario.txt`](../scenario.txt); anisotropic, 16 neighbours, no
limit.

- AccessMod 5: only its engine ([`am5.sh`](am5.sh)): one
  `r.walk.accessmod` per origin, stopped once every destination is reached
  (`stop_points`), `AM5_JOBS` (9) at a time with `AM5_MEMORY_MB` (2000)
  each. The `r.path` and `v.net` distance steps and the R orchestration are
  not run: this is a **lower bound** of an AccessMod 5 referral.
- accessmod-rs: the whole `referral run` command (times and distances);
  `rs solve` is its search phase.

## How to read it

- On the demo, AccessMod 5 spends most of its time in the distance steps
  and R, not in the searches: compare the demo row for what users wait for,
  the synthetic rows for how the engines scale.
- Times agree with AccessMod 5 on the demo referral (see
  `docs/reference-accessmod5.md`); distances differ by less than one cell
  diagonal (cell centres vs `r.path` lines from the points).
- With fewer destinations than origins, accessmod-rs runs as many searches
  as destinations (`search_from: auto`); `nearest_only` stops each search
  early. Neither is used here.
