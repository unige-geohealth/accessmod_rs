# Geographic coverage benchmark

AccessMod 5 against accessmod-rs: facilities take, one after the other,
the population they reach up to their capacity. Results:
[`report.md`](report.md), generated by:

```sh
sh benchmark/geographic-coverage/run.sh [side facilities ...]   # default: 1000 20 2000 50 4000 100
```

Needs Docker, the AccessMod 5 image `fredmoser/accessmod:5.9.2-beta.6` and,
for the demo part, the oracle exports (`sh scripts/oracle/export.sh`).
`REPEAT` (default 3) sets the runs per measurement; the report gives the
median. Scratch files go to `_shared/benchmark/geographic-coverage/`.

## What is measured

**Demo project, full analysis.** The AccessMod 5 demo (227 × 196 cells,
10 facilities ordered by capacity, anisotropic, towards facilities,
8 neighbours, 120 min, population removed at each step, catchments):

- AccessMod 5: the replay `scripts/oracle/capacity-demo.json` (from
  `replay/examples/loop_capacity_stat`) run by `amAnalysisReplayExec`.
  Loading `global.R` is reported apart.
- accessmod-rs: `geographic-coverage run` on the same data
  (`demo-job.json`): capacity table, residual population, catchment
  polygons and reports.

**Synthetic landscapes, engine.** `side × side` cells of 100 m from
`synthetic_inputs`: population 50 per cell in the town, 5 elsewhere,
`facilities` facilities of capacity 20 000 on the main road, the scenario
of [`../scenario.txt`](../scenario.txt); anisotropic, towards facilities,
16 neighbours, 120 min, catchments written.

- AccessMod 5: only its engine ([`am5.sh`](am5.sh)): one
  `r.walk.accessmod` per facility limited to 120 min, one after the other
  (each facility depends on the residual population left by the previous
  ones). The minute rounding, band sums, ring removal, catchment polygons
  and R orchestration are not run: this is a **lower bound** of an
  AccessMod 5 coverage.
- accessmod-rs: the whole `geographic-coverage run` command; `rs coverage`
  is its facility loop (searches, catchments, removal), `Cells reached`
  the cells visited by all searches.

## How to read it

- Both engines are sequential over facilities; accessmod-rs gains from its
  solver and from doing band sums, removal and polygons on the reached
  cells only, where AccessMod 5 runs raster operations over the whole
  computational region for every facility.
- Results agree with AccessMod 5 on the demo replays (table, residual
  population, catchments; see `docs/geographic-coverage.md`).
